How I Work

  1. 1

    Understand the Problem

    Talk to customers, users, and stakeholders, and look at the data. Agree on the problem and how success will be measured before proposing a solution.

  2. 2

    Define & Prioritize

    Turn what was learned into a roadmap and a backlog ordered by value and effort, with the reasoning visible.

  3. 3

    Build With the Team

    Work with engineering and design every day: refine, answer questions fast, make trade-offs, and accept work against clear criteria.

  4. 4

    Launch & Adoption

    Plan the release, prepare the people who will use and support it, and make sure it lands with the audience it was built for.

  5. 5

    Measure & Iterate

    Check the results against the goal, share what happened plainly, and feed it into what gets built next.
